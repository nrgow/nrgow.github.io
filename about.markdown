I'm **Nicholas Gow**, an NLP / applied-LLM engineer based in Berlin. For about ten
years I've built data-heavy language systems and dragged them from prototype all the
way to production, usually somewhere in the messy overlap of speech, multilingual NLU,
and large-scale data pipelines.

This site is where I write up experiments and notes: small, self-contained projects
in applied LLM work, speech, and entity linking. Less polished paper, more lab notebook.

### What I work on

- **Applied LLMs**: fine-tuning (SFT), synthetic-data generation, ASR error correction,
  vector search and retrieval.
- **Multilingual NLU & entity recognition**: the hard, code-switched, real-world kind.
- **Speech**: spoken-language entity linking, phonetic embeddings, TTS-driven synthetic data.
- **Data engineering**: the streaming pipelines and infrastructure that make the above
  actually run (Spark, Kafka, ClickHouse, SageMaker, Triton, vLLM, Kubernetes).

### A bit of background

Most recently I was **Senior Engineer in Algorithm Research at NIO** (2022 to 2025), where I
owned the media domain for the NOMI in-car voice assistant: multilingual entity
recognition, NLU classifier pipelines, and a low-latency LLM for ASR error correction.
Before that I spent four years at **YUKKA Lab** building real-time financial-news
sentiment infrastructure (knowledge graphs, streaming ingestion, search & ranking APIs),
and got my start in data engineering at **TrustYou** in Munich. I hold a **BA in
Computational Linguistics** from the University of Tübingen.
